# ICP Avatar — Prime Minds

## Demographics
- **Gender:** Male
- **Age:** 23-31
- **Location:** Morocco (primarily Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech)
- **Education:** Master's degree (5 years), few years of work experience
- **Relationship:** Single/has girlfriend, or just broke up. Not married.
- **Income:** 10,000-17,000 MAD/month (fixed salary)
- **Savings:** Zero or minimal (gets spent on travel or lifestyle)

## Psychographics
- **Platforms:** Instagram, TikTok daily. YouTube for longer content.
- **Content consumed:** Self-development, football, streamers, funny reels
- **Activities:** Likes going out, socializing, staying active
- **Language:** Darija (Moroccan Arabic) with French/English technical terms

## Current Situation (Pain)
- Fixed salary, no business model to start
- Confused and frustrated
- Tired of job and the feeling of not having money
- Very close to giving up
- Feeling stuck — "life is passing by quickly"

## Desired Situation (Dream)
- 50,000 MAD/month consistently
- Nice car (BMW Serie 4 / Audi A3 S-Line) paid cash
- Get married without financial anxiety
- Travel outside Morocco consistently
- Buy gifts for family, retire parents
- Savings without sacrificing lifestyle

## What They Truly Want
- Freedom and time with family
- Feeling secure
- Less stress
- Pride in building something real
- Being a provider his family can rely on

## Past Attempts (Failed)
- **Ecom/Dropshipping:** Tight margins, too much hassle
- **Trading/Forex:** Put in $10-$100, saw small profits, lost everything
- **SMMA:** Knew about it but seemed like too much work alongside job
- Some tried but couldn't balance work and business

## Limiting Beliefs
- "It's impossible to build a business while working full-time"
- "I can't sell to businesses if I never did"
- "How can I sell something if I don't have proof?"
- "People are making money online, but there's something they're hiding"
- "The only way to make money is illegal at this point"
- "I just need to live life and forget about money"
- "Everyone has their rizq written — I don't have to fight"
- "You can't start a business without large capital"
- "I can't start without being a master in skills"
- "I need to show my face to make money online"

## What Messaging Works
- **Mirror their language** — speak like a friend, not a guru
- **Share failures** — shows you understand because you've been there
- **Anti-guru positioning** — "I'm not here to sell you a dream"
- **Cultural references** — Darija phrases, Moroccan lifestyle context
- **Real numbers** — actual income, actual costs, actual timelines
- **Emotional triggers:** Marriage anxiety, family responsibility, feeling stuck

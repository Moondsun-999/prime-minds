## Channel Roles

| Channel | Role | Frequency | CTA Target |
|---------|------|-----------|------------|
| YouTube Shorts | Awareness + Interest | Daily | YouTube channel |
| IG Reels | Awareness + Interest | Daily | YouTube channel |
| TikTok | Awareness + Interest | Daily | YouTube channel |
| X / Threads | Engagement + Reach | Daily | YouTube videos |

| Landing Page | Conversion | Always on | Skool community |
| Skool | Retention + Upsell | Always on | Coaching tier |

## Key Metrics
- **Top of funnel:** Views, reach, follower growth
- **Mid funnel:** Click-through to YouTube, watch time, subscriber growth
- **Bottom funnel:** Landing page visits, Skool signups, payment conversions
- **Retention:** Weekly active members, call attendance, module completion

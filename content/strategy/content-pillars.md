# Content Pillars — Prime Minds

## 5 Pillars

### 1. Journey Documentation (30%)
**Core idea:** Building in public, documenting real wins and failures
**Topics:**
- AI agency progress updates
- Revenue milestones (or lack thereof)
- Tool stack and workflow reveals
- "Today I..." daily updates on X/Threads
**Why:** Builds trust, creates parasocial connection, proves authenticity

### 2. Education (25%)
**Core idea:** Teaching valuable skills — AI, business, mindset
**Topics:**
- Claude Code tutorials
- AI automation (n8n, Make)
- Business fundamentals
- Prompt engineering
- Offer creation
**Why:** Attracts ICP through search (YouTube SEO), positions as expert

### 3. Comparison & Analysis (20%)
**Core idea:** Honest analysis of business models and tools
**Topics:**
- COD vs Trading vs AI Agency
- Tool comparisons
- Business model breakdowns
- Morocco-specific challenges
**Why:** High search volume, differentiates from gurus who only push one model

### 4. Motivation & Mindset (15%)
**Core idea:** Paradigm shifts, mental models, discipline
**Topics:**
- Money principles (energy, deservingness, security)
- Comfort zone & discipline
- Breaking analysis paralysis
- "Why most people will never make money online"
**Why:** Emotional resonance, shareable, connects with ICP beliefs

### 5. Community & Social Proof (10%)
**Core idea:** Member wins, community highlights, calls
**Topics:**
- Member success stories
- Weekly call highlights
- Community Q&A compilations
- Behind-the-scenes of community
**Why:** Converts viewers to members, shows the community is real

# Video Production Workflow

## Long-Form Process (1 video/week)
1. **Ideation** — Pull from content pillars + trending topics + community questions
2. **Headline** — Clear, benefit-driven, curiosity-inducing (Darija + English mix)
3. **Hook** — First 10 seconds script. Must pass the "would I stop scrolling?" test
4. **Body** — 3-5 segments with transitions, screen recordings, personal stories
5. **CTA** — Drive to community (Skool), subscribe, next video tease
6. **Thumbnail** — 2-3 words max, expressive face, brand colors (#360A97 + #DA8690)
7. **Edit** — Jump cuts, B-roll, text overlays, music
8. **Post** — YouTube SEO: title, description, tags, chapters, end screen

## Short-Form Process (1 video/day)
1. **Ideation** — Extract from long-form video or standalone insight
2. **Hook** — 3-second stop-scroller (text on screen + verbal)
3. **Body** — One point, fast delivery, 20-40 seconds
4. **CTA** — "Follow for more" / "Link in bio" / "Full video on YouTube"
5. **Edit** — Captions, zoom cuts, sound effects
6. **Post** — YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok (same video, slight edits per platform)

## Hook Formula Bank
- **Contrarian:** "Kolchi kaygoulek [popular advice]. Ana kangoulek [opposite]."
- **Pain-point:** "Wach nta khedam 9-5 o mabghitich tbqa haka?"
- **Story:** "Smiti Abdellah, o had shi li wqa3 lia..."
- **Statistic:** "15,000 dh f Casablanca? Nta f akhtar trap."
- **Challenge:** "Goul lia f les commentaires wach true ola false..."

## Thumbnail Best Practices
- Face with strong emotion (shock, excitement, determination)
- 2-3 words in bold text (Playfair Display or bold sans)
- Brand gradient overlay or color accent
- High contrast, readable at mobile size
- Never: cluttered, small text, stock photos

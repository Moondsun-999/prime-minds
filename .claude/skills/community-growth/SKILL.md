# Community Growth (Skool)

## Community Identity
- **Platform:** Skool
- **Name:** Prime Minds
- **Mission:** Serious people building businesses while working 9-to-5
- **Culture:** Anti-guru, action-oriented, accountability-driven

## Growth Flywheel
```
YouTube Content → Free Skool Access → Member Wins → Social Proof → More YouTube Content
```

## Member Activation
1. **Welcome message** within 1 hour of joining
2. **Introduction post** prompt — "Tell us your situation + goal"
3. **First module** recommendation based on their intro
4. **Accountability partner** pairing after 1 week
5. **First win** celebration within 30 days

## Engagement Levers
- **Weekly calls:** Sunday, consistent time, always have an agenda
- **Daily threads:** Rotate through engagement calendar
- **Progress tracking:** Monthly "where are you now?" posts
- **Exclusive content:** Share things in community FIRST, then YouTube later
- **Member spotlights:** Feature wins publicly (with permission)

## Retention Metrics
- **Day 1:** Did they introduce themselves?
- **Week 1:** Did they complete Module 1?
- **Month 1:** Did they attend a call?
- **Month 3:** Are they posting / engaging weekly?

## Referral Strategy
- Ask happy members to share on social
- Create shareable community highlight graphics
- "Bring a friend" challenges with rewards (extra 1:1 time, shoutouts)

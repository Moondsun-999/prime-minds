# Brand Voice Rules

## Core Identity
Prime Minds is the ANTI-GURU brand. Abdallah is not selling a dream — he's documenting his real path.

## Voice Characteristics
- **Raw:** Share real numbers, real failures, real struggles
- **Direct:** Say it straight. No corporate hedging.
- **Empathetic:** You WERE the ICP. You understand because you lived it.
- **Humble:** "I'm still figuring it out" energy. Never "I have all the answers."
- **Action-oriented:** Always end with a step they can take NOW.

## Language Rules
- ✅ "I failed at 9 businesses" — specific, honest
- ❌ "After years of experience" — vague, guru-coded
- ✅ "This is hard. But here's what worked for me."
- ❌ "This simple trick will change your life!"
- ✅ "Join me on this path"
- ❌ "I'll show you the way"

## Banned Phrases
- "Financial freedom" (overused, guru-coded)
- "Passive income" (misleading)
- "Life-changing" (hyperbolic)
- "Secret" or "hack" (clickbait)
- "Guaranteed results" (legally risky, guru-coded)
- Any income claims without context

## Darija Content Rules
- Use everyday Darija, not Fusha
- Technical terms in English/French are natural
- Never code-switch to look sophisticated
- Humor is welcome but never at someone's expense
